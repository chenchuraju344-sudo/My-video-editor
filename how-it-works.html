How DoBlood.online Works

1

Register as Donor

Click Become Donor, enter your Name, Blood Group, Pincode, Area and enable Live Location. Your profile becomes active across India instantly.

2

Find Donors in 5-20 KM Radius

Go to Find Donors, login with your donor account, select Blood Group, enter Area/Pincode and select Radius. Our system filters donors using GPS distance calculation and shows only nearby active donors.

3

Emergency Alert Ticker

If you need blood urgently, click Send Alert, enter Patient Name, Blood Group, Hospital. Your alert will scroll live on homepage ticker for all users and a push notification is sent.

4

Manage & Privacy

In Manage Profile, you can turn OFF your availability if you have donated recently, update location, or permanently delete your account.
