Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026 | Effective for DoBlood.online App & Website

1. What Data We Collect: We collect Name, Phone Number, Email, Blood Group, Age, Area, Pincode, and optional GPS coordinates (lat/lng) when you register as a donor. This is essential to connect you with a patient nearby.

2. How We Use Data: Your data is used ONLY to show your profile to verified users searching for blood in your area. We do NOT sell, rent, or share your phone number with third-party advertisers.

3. Data Storage: All data is securely stored in Google Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud) with encrypted transmission (HTTPS). Passwords are stored securely.

4. Privacy Control: You have an option "Hide contact phone number publicly". If enabled, your number is hidden and others must request contact via app.

5. Data Retention: Data is retained until you delete your profile via Manage Profile > Terminate Profile. Upon deletion, all data is permanently removed from Firebase within 24 hours.

6. Children's Privacy: This service is not for users under 18 years of age.

7. Contact: For any privacy concerns, contact us at support@doblood.online
