Non-Commercial Disclaimer

DoBlood.online operates strictly as a FREE, non-commercial, autonomous emergency bridge connecting voluntary blood donors with patients in need.

1. No Sale of Blood: As per The Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and National Blood Policy of India, sale or purchase of human blood or blood components is strictly prohibited and is a punishable offense. Our platform does not support or allow any financial transaction for blood.

2. No Charges: We do not charge any fee from donors or recipients. If anyone asks you for money in the name of DoBlood.online, it is fraud. Please report to support@doblood.online immediately.

3. Verification: We try our best to keep genuine donors, but we cannot physically verify every donor. Users are advised to verify donor identity at hospital before transfusion.

By using this platform, you agree that you will not use this platform for any commercial or illegal purpose.
