System Operational Guidelines

DoBlood.online Protocol & Ethical Mandate

Platform Execution Steps

  • Register as Donor: Execute "Become Donor", provide bio-metrics & set an encrypted password.
  • Locate Donors: To enforce anti-spam protocols, login credentials are mandatory to execute search queries.
  • Emergency Alerts: Dispatch urgent requests from the dashboard to broadcast on the network live header ticker.
  • Profile Management: Navigate to "Manage Profile" to toggle live status, alter location metrics, or terminate records.

Non-Commercial Disclaimer:

DoBlood.online operates strictly as a non-commercial, autonomous emergency bridge connecting voluntary blood donors with patients. Commercialization or monetized trading of human blood is strictly prohibited by law. DoBlood.online disclaims all liability for unauthorized financial solicitations.

Privacy Policy

At DoBlood.online, safeguarding your privacy is our top priority. This policy outlines how user information is collected, encrypted, and utilized across our network.

1. Information Collection

We collect basic voluntary information including Name, Mobile Phone Number, Blood Group, Region/Pincode, Email, and optional Geo-Location metrics.

2. Zero-Spam Data Protection

Donor contact details are protected behind authentication barriers. Search operations require authorized donor credentials to prevent automated bots and spam marketing calls.

3. Data Usage

Your details are strictly used to assist critical medical requests. We never sell, monetize, or rent personal data to commercial third parties.

Terms & Conditions

By accessing or using DoBlood.online, you agree to comply with the following mandatory operational terms:

1. Voluntary Non-Commercial Platform

Blood donation must be entirely voluntary and non-remunerated. Demanding or offering money for blood donations on this platform is strictly illegal.

2. User Verification & Responsibility

Users are solely responsible for ensuring the accuracy of submitted bio-metrics. Platform admins reserve the right to remove suspicious profiles instantly.
