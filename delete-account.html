Delete Account & Data

Required for Google Play Store Data Safety compliance

How to delete your donor account from DoBlood.online:

  1. Open DoBlood.online website or app.
  2. Click Menu > Manage Profile.
  3. Login with your registered Phone Number and Password.
  4. After login, scroll down and click Terminate Profile button.
  5. Confirm deletion. Your entire profile, including Name, Phone, Email, Location will be permanently deleted from our Firebase database within 24 hours.

Alternative Method: If you cannot login, send an email to support@doblood.online from your registered email ID with subject "Delete My Account - [Your Phone Number]". We will delete within 48 hours.

What data is deleted? All personal data: name, phone, email, age, area, pincode, lat/lng, password. No data is retained after deletion.
